# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Affluent travelers — couples, families, and celebration groups — planning a high-end villa stay in Mexico. They arrive from Instagram, referrals, or search, usually comparing against hotel suites and marketplace listings. Their context: dreaming and vetting on mobile, booking decisions made on desktop. The job to be done: feel certain that this villa, this team, and this destination will deliver a flawless, private, indulgent trip — and get a fast, personal answer when they reach out.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for Mexico Luxe Stays, a luxury villa rental brand operating in Playa del Carmen (Riviera Maya) and Valle de Guadalupe (Baja California wine country). It showcases the villa portfolio and concierge services, and converts visitors into direct booking inquiries (contact form / WhatsApp). Live availability, pricing, and booking will later be powered by the Hostaway API. Success looks like qualified inquiries: visitors who have already chosen a villa and dates before they ever message.

## Positioning

Where luxury, comfort, and exclusivity meet in two of Mexico's most breathtaking destinations — beachfront Riviera Maya and Baja wine country, under one host with 20+ years of experience.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: book / send an inquiry (contact form, WhatsApp click-to-chat).
- Secondary CTA: browse the villa collection; follow on Instagram for visitors not ready to commit.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Where luxury, comfort, and exclusivity meet."
- Belief ladder: (1) these villas are genuinely extraordinary → (2) both destinations are worth the trip → (3) this team delivers personal, flawless service end-to-end (20+ years, staff mentioned by name in reviews) → (4) booking direct with them is easy and safe.
- Proof on hand: guest testimonials in the style of real reviews (warm, specific, naming staff by first name — Carlos, Able); 20+ years managing luxury vacation properties. Real testimonial feed and Hostaway data to be integrated later (placeholder markers in code).

## Brand Personality

Bold, glamorous, exclusive — VIP members-club energy. Confident, short copy that never begs; the site behaves like it doesn't need to convince you. Dramatic full-bleed photography, deep green dominance, oversized display type. Warmth appears in the details (staff names, personal service) rather than in exclamation points.

## Anti-references

- Generic booking marketplaces (Airbnb, Booking.com): dense card grids, price-first UI, cluttered filter chrome.
- Loud resort/travel-deal sites: bright turquoise-and-orange palettes, starburst discounts, "BOOK NOW!!" urgency banners, countdown timers.
- Cold corporate real-estate/property-management sites: gray palettes, stocky suits, jargon copy.

## Design Principles

1. **Let the photograph be the design.** Full-bleed imagery leads every fold; UI recedes to frame it.
2. **The arch is the signature.** The logo's double-arch recurs as an image mask and shape language — instantly ours, no one else's.
3. **Dark green is the velvet rope.** Deep brand green (#3A4638) sections signal exclusivity; cream sections let the portfolio breathe.
4. **Service is personal.** Copy names people and specifics — a private chef, Carlos adjusting the jacuzzi — never "world-class amenities" boilerplate.
5. **One idea per fold.** Deliberate pacing, oversized type, generous space. Never three messages competing in one viewport.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target: contrast-checked palette (cream on brand green passes AA), visible focus states on all interactive elements, semantic landmarks and heading order, alt text written in brand voice, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (entrance animation and carousels degrade to static).
