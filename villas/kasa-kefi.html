Kasa Kefi

Between the vines

Four suites with private fire-pit terraces, an infinity plunge pool above the vines. A wine cellar waiting downstairs.

Kasa Kefi opens itself to the valley — floor-to-ceiling glass, seamless indoor-outdoor living, a loft living room with an ethanol fireplace, and a dining room built around its own wine cellar. On the terrace: al fresco dining, a BBQ, sun beds, and fire pits pointed at the sunset.

Every suite comes with an ensuite bath, plush bedding, and its own lounge terrace; comforts run from Starlink Wi-Fi and Sonos sound to Nespresso in the mornings. Acclaimed wineries, Michelin-starred dining, and hiking trails are all a short drive from the gate.
